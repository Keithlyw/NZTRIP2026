🎒 Smart Packing List

🌤️ Weather in December (NZ summer): The South Island in mid-December is pleasant and sunny but changeable. Expect warm days of about 18–25 °C and cool evenings around 8–12 °C, especially near Lake Tekapo, Mount Cook and Queenstown. Mountain and lake areas can get chilly and windy, the sun is very strong (high UV), and a quick shower is always possible. In short: pack layers, sun protection, and a light rain jacket.
